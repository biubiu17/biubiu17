**Tyee** supports research with physiological signals such as EEG, ECG and EMG. Its configurable components connect data preparation,
model training and evaluation, making it easier to compare methods across datasets and tasks.

The toolkit accompanies a paper by **Tao Zhou, Lingyu Shu, Zixing Zhang and Jing Han** at **ACM Multimedia 2025**.
It is a collaboration involving Hunan University and the University of Cambridge.

- [Paper and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746027.3756868)
- [Source code and documentation](https://github.com/SmileHnu/Tyee)

Please consult the original repository for installation, supported datasets and licensing.
