**OPERA** brings together respiratory recordings and evaluation tasks to study reusable audio representations for health research.
The project provides pretrained models and benchmarks covering signals such as breathing, coughing and lung sounds.

The paper **Towards Open Respiratory Acoustic Foundation Models: Pretraining and Benchmarking** appeared in the
**NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track**, with Jing Han as a coauthor.
This work was developed with the Mobile Systems Research Lab at the University of Cambridge.

- [Project website and paper](https://opera-benchmark.github.io/)
- [Source code and model resources](https://github.com/evelyn0414/OPERA)

Dataset availability and access conditions are described by the original project.
