HanLab

AI for healthcare, audio & non-audio learning · Hunan University

HanLab is a research group at the School of Computer Science, Hunan University, led by Professor Jing Han (韩静). We study how artificial intelligence can learn from audio and other human signals to support health and wellbeing.

Professor Jing Han

Role & research. Jing Han is a professor in the School of Computer Science at Hunan University and leads HanLab. Her research connects healthcare with audio, physiological signals, multimodal learning and deep learning.

Education & research experience. She received her PhD from the University of Augsburg, Germany, in 2020, supervised by Professor Björn W. Schuller. She subsequently conducted postdoctoral research on wearable sensing and mobile health at the University of Cambridge, working with Professor Cecilia Mascolo. She received her master’s degree from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, in 2014, and her bachelor’s degree in Electronic and Information Engineering from Harbin Engineering University, China, in 2011.

Academic service. She serves as an Associate Editor of IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing and reviews for IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, IEEE Signal Processing Magazine and IEEE Transactions on Multimedia. She co-organised special sessions at ICASSP 2022 and INTERSPEECH 2021, and has served as a session chair or programme committee member at international conferences.

Selected recognition. Honourable Mention, AAAC Outstanding PhD Dissertation Award (2021); member of the Cambridge research team receiving the Better Future Award (2021).
