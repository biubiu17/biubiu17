HanLab

AI for health, audio & multimodal understanding · Hunan University

HanLab is a research group at the School of Computer Science, Hunan University, led by Professor Jing Han (韩静). We study how artificial intelligence can learn from audio and other human signals to support health and wellbeing.

湖南大学计算机学院 · HanLab

What we study

  • Intelligent healthcare: mobile health, respiratory and cardiac sound analysis, and physiological signal understanding.
  • Audio & non-audio learning: representation learning across speech, sounds and other sensing modalities.
  • Multimodal affective computing: emotion recognition and sentiment understanding from complementary signals.

Explore our research and collaborative projects and publications, including earlier work by Jing Han and collaborators.
