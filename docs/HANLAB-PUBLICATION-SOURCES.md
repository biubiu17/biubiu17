## Editorial decisions and additional checks

- `Han2026SleepApnea`: [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42009325/) gives online publication as **2026-04-20** and labels the paper ahead of print. Crossref has year 2026 and pages 1-14 but no volume/issue; the bibliography retains an **Early access** note instead of inventing these fields.
- `Han2026OpenVocabularyEmotion`: the [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666651026000112) and Crossref confirm **AI Open 7, 194-207 (2026)**. The final title ends in **Towards open vocabularies**. The [2025 arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20938) has the earlier subtitle **From Closed Sets to Open Vocabularies**; it is linked from the same entry rather than counted as a second paper. A precise final publication day was not verified; neither Crossref deposit dates nor the `2026.06` DOI segment are treated as publication dates.
- `Zhu2025AudioFab`, `Zhou2025Tyee`, and `Gao2025StructuredPrompting`: ACM and Crossref state publication on **2025-10-27**. The proceedings versions are used, even though arXiv copies were posted later.
- `Zhang2025OnDeviceEmotion`: the DOI contains 2024, but the final journal issue is **12(4), 4169-4182 (2025)**.
- `Li2025CognitiveState`: the [University of Edinburgh publication record](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/semi-supervised-cognitive-state-classification-from-speech-with-m/) confirms ICASSP 2025 and the DOI. The 2024 arXiv copy is linked from the final conference entry.
- `Zhang2024OPERA`: checked against the [official NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/2f803abdcad9de35b45d5a656dade45c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html). The proceedings spell one author's given name **Yu Yvonne Wu**, which is retained rather than the shorter **Yu Wu** from Crossref. The venue is the NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.
- `Han2022SoundsOfCOVID`: the [Nature publisher page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-021-00553-x) states publication on **2022-01-28**, volume 5, **article 16**. The article number is used in the bibliography's `pages` field for compatibility with the site's citation style.
- `Han2021MobileMentalHealth`: author identity and ordering were also checked in the [Cambridge accepted manuscript](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/e3da4603-677e-43f4-8dda-0332a4fd214e/content). Diacritics in Elisabeth Andre and Bjorn W. Schuller were restored to match that manuscript.
- `Han2021EmoBed` and `Kossaifi2021SEWA`: their DOIs contain 2019, but the bibliography uses the final **2021** journal issues.
- The preprint DOI `10.2196/preprints.37004` is not imported separately from the final JMIR article `10.2196/37004`.
- The discovered **Machine Unlearning in Audio** submission is not described as an accepted ICLR 2025 paper: its [OpenReview author listing](https://openreview.net/profile?id=~Helen_Yannakoudakis1) distinguishes a submission from publication. It was omitted from this initial selection.
